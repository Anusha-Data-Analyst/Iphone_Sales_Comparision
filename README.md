# Iphone_Sales_Comparision
- # 🔍Project Overview: iPhone 14 vs. iPhone 15
The focus is on evaluating performance shifts, revenue growth, and model-on-model performance using key variance metrics.

- # 🎯Key Project Metrics & Calculations
 - **Sales Value:** Measures the total revenue generated by each model line across its respective variants.
  - **Absolute Sales Variance:** Calculates the absolute numerical difference in sales revenue between the iPhone 14 and iPhone 15 {Absolute Sales Variance = ABS([Iphone 15 Sales]-[Iphone 14 Sales])}
  - **Sales Variance %:** Determines the percentage growth or decline in sales value from the iPhone 14 to the iPhone 15 {Sales Variance % = ([Iphone 15 Sales]-[Iphone 14 Sales])/[Iphone 14 Sales]}
  - # Executive Key Takeaways
  - **Overall Global Decline:** Total iPhone sales declined by -4.26% overall, dropping from 1,232,065 units (iPhone 14) to 1,179,524 units (iPhone 15), representing a total absolute variance of 52,541 units.
  -  **Top Growth Markets:**
    
   - - **Brazil** recorded the highest relative growth rate at +19.69% (an absolute gain of 14,446 units).
     
   - - **Germany** drove the largest volume increase, growing +15.51% (+30,126 units).
     
   - - **India** demonstrated strong momentum with +11.26% growth (+16,630 units).

 -  **Major Declining Markets:**

   - - **United States** experienced the sharpest drop both in percentage (-32.97%) and volume, selling 88,731 fewer units.
   - - **UAE** saw a noticeable contraction of -15.30% (-30,956 units).
